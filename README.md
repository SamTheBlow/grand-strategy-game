## Grand Strategy Maker
[Download the latest release](https://github.com/SamTheBlow/grand-strategy-maker/releases/latest)

Play a grand strategy game, or make your own and share it!
You can use this as a sandbox, as a game engine, or just to play games.

- Edit entire projects and save files
- Random world generation
- Local and online multiplayer
- Chat and chat commands
- Different AI types and personalities

Note that it is still in early development: graphics, audio and tons of features are lacking.
